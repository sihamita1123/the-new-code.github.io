Part 2 • Concept & Brand

Our Concept

What is Simple Delicious Snacks, and who is it for? Here is the big idea in three pieces.

Simple Delicious Snacks brings the street markets of East Africa — from Mogadishu to Mombasa — to our school hallway. Warm spices, crispy fried snacks, and ice-cold mango smoothies, served fast and friendly. It is a pop-up micro-food stall: small, temporary, and full of flavour.
🎨

Theme & Vibe

A mini East African street market: warm terracotta and gold colours, a market-stall style counter, a chalkboard menu, and an African music playlist. Fun, colourful, and welcoming — you feel the market the second you walk up.

🤲

Service Style

Counter service & grab-and-go. You order at the window and get your food hot in 2–3 minutes — perfect for a short lunch break. Morning pre-orders skip the line completely.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Target Market

High school students & teachers — especially athletes who need snacks after practice, students who keep halal (our beef is halal), adventurous eaters, and anyone with $5 who wants something better than chips.

The Menu

Three items, made fresh, priced for students. Snack, snack, and a drink — the perfect lunch combo.

Snack

Beef Sanbusa

$4.002 pieces
⭐ Most Popular • Halal

Crispy golden pastry triangles stuffed with juicy spiced ground beef and sweet onions — the king of East African street snacks.

What's inside

How we make it

Cook the spiced beef filling → fold into triangles → deep-fry until golden and crunchy → drain → serve hot.

Snack

Viazi Karai

$4.006 pieces
🌱 100% Vegetarian

Crispy fried potato bites in a golden spiced chickpea-flour batter, served with a tangy tamarind (ukwaju) dip — street food straight from the coast of Kenya.

What's inside

Potatoes • Chickpea (gram) flour • Garlic • Chilli • Turmeric • Tamarind sauce

How we make it

Boil & cube the potatoes → dip in spiced batter → deep-fry until crispy outside, soft inside → serve with dip.

Part 1 • Industry Terms Research

10 Words Every Food Business Uses

Each term is defined in simple words, then shown the way we actually use it at Simple Delicious Snacks.

🌐

Term 1

Tourism

When people travel to new places for fun, culture, or food — and spend money while they are there.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

Food is one of the top reasons travellers explore a new culture. Our stall is like a mini trip to East Africa for students, teachers, and visitors at school events.

🤝

Term 2

Hospitality

Making guests feel welcome and taking good care of them from the moment they arrive.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

We greet every customer with a smile, keep the stall spotless, and make the wait fun with music and the smell of fresh sanbusa.

🍽️

Term 3

Food & Beverage (F&B) Operations

All the work behind making and serving food and drinks — prepping, cooking, serving, and cleaning up.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

Every lunch hour we prep ingredients, fry sanbusa and viazi karai fresh, blend mango smoothies, serve customers fast, and clean everything before the next class.

🎯

Term 4

Target Market

The specific group of people a business wants to sell to.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

High school students and teachers who want a quick, tasty lunch under $5 — especially athletes after practice, students who eat halal, and anyone who loves new flavours.

😊

Term 5

Customer Experience (GX / CX)

How a customer feels during every step of buying from you — before, during, and after.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

Fast service so nobody misses lunch, a cold mango smoothie after gym class, clear prices, and a friendly "see you tomorrow!" so every student leaves happy and comes back.

🏪

Term 6

Pop-Up Retail / Micro-Food Stall

A small, temporary shop that opens for a short time instead of renting a permanent space.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

Simple Delicious Snacks is a pop-up! We set up at lunch, school events, and sports days — low cost, no lease, and we go where the customers are.

📊

Term 7

Menu Engineering

Designing your menu with smart placement, photos, and pricing so the most profitable items sell the most.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

Our "star" item is the Beef Sanbusa — popular AND profitable — so it sits at the top of the menu with a big photo. The $2 mango smoothie is the low-cost add-on that makes our combo deal an easy yes.

🧮

Term 8

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

The total cost of the ingredients and materials used to make the food you sell.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

One beef sanbusa costs us about $0.60 in ingredients (pastry, beef, onion, spices, oil). We sell it for $4.00, so we keep about $3.40. Knowing our COGS helps us price fairly and still make a profit.

🛡️

Term 9

Food Safety & Sanitation Standards

Official rules — like Alberta Health Services guidelines and Foodsafe training — that keep food clean and safe to eat.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

We follow AHS basics: wash hands and wear gloves, tie hair back, keep raw beef away from ready-to-eat food, keep cold food under 4°C and hot food over 60°C, and sanitize every surface. (Full plan below!)

✨

Term 10

Value Proposition

The special promise that makes customers choose you instead of somewhere else.

At Simple Delicious Snacks

"Real East African street food, made fresh at school, for under $5." No one else in our school — or the mall — offers that.

Part 3 • Operations & Tourism Plan

How We Run the Stall

Tourism, safety, and marketing — the plan behind the food.

🌐

Tourism Connection

Food is the fastest way to experience another culture — that is the heart of tourism.

  • Food tourism is huge — travellers plan whole trips around what they will eat.
  • Our school welcomes international students, families, and visitors — Simple Delicious Snacks gives them a real taste of East Africa without leaving the building.
  • Every dish has a story: sanbusa travelled ancient trade routes from the Middle East and India to the Horn of Africa — we print that story on our stall sign.
  • At cultural nights we hand out "food passport" cards with one fun fact about each dish — a mini vacation for your taste buds.
🛡️

Food Safety & Hygiene

We follow Alberta Health Services food safety basics — no shortcuts.

  • Wash hands for 20 seconds before prep, after raw meat, and after touching your face or phone; wear gloves for ready-to-eat food.
  • Hair tied back, clean apron, no jewellery — anyone feeling sick stays home.
  • No cross-contamination: separate boards & containers for raw beef; raw meat always on the bottom fridge shelf.
  • Temperature control: fridge at or below 4°C, hot food held above 60°C, beef cooked fully — no pink inside.
  • Fryer safety: dry potatoes before frying, never leave hot oil unattended, keep a lid close by.
  • Allergens labelled (gluten in pastry, milk in the mango smoothie) and we always ask about allergies before serving.
  • Clean-as-you-go: sanitize counters before & after service, wash dishes in hot soapy water, take out garbage daily.
📣

Marketing Plan

Get students excited before we even open the window.

  • Posters with big food photos, prices, and our stall location — up one week before launch day.
  • Morning announcements + free mini sanbusa samples on launch day to create buzz.
  • Instagram & TikTok: @SimpleDeliciousSnacks — daily menu stories, behind-the-scenes cooking videos, customer shout-outs.
  • Combo deal (Sanbusa + Mango Smoothie for $5.50) and a loyalty card: buy 5 lunches, get a free smoothie.
  • Pre-order slips in homeroom so students with short breaks skip the line.
  • Word of mouth — happy students are the best advertising we could ask for.
